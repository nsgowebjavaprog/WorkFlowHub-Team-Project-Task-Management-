How it works

Three steps from resume to result.

1. Sign up

Create an account as a candidate or a recruiter with a secure JWT-based login.

2. Add your resume

Paste your resume text and browse open job postings from recruiters.

3. Get matched

Click "check my match" — the AI service returns a score and the exact skill gaps to fix.

Built with Django · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · Three.js — a portfolio project.
